Sanskrit Speech Analyzer Guide

🎤 Speech & Audio Recording

Click the microphone button to start recording. Speak any Sanskrit word clearly. Click again to finalize, or select any preset word.

📊 Real-time Visualizations

The waveform shows time-domain energy fluctuations. The spectrogram calculates frequency intensity (0-8 kHz) in real-time.

🔤 Phonetic & Matra Decomposition

Words are decomposed into their true consonant and vowel components (e.g. शिव → श + इ + व + अ) according to classical Shiksha Shastra.

👅 Articulatory Sthāna (Points of Origin)

Each phoneme is classified into its traditional place of articulation: Kaṇṭhya (Throat), Tālavya (Palate), Mūrdhanya (Roof), Dantya (Teeth), Oṣṭhya (Lips), and Nāsikya (Nose).
